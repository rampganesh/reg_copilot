# No Feedback Loop on Answers

## Statement

"The team currently has no consistent way of recording whether an answer produced by previous research was useful, whether it was subsequently corrected, or which source ultimately resolved the question." Analysts "sometimes discover after several weeks that a previous answer was based on an outdated document or incomplete interpretation. There is no systematic feedback mechanism for capturing this information." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L63, L65)**

## Who has it

- **Regulatory Reporting Manager**: identifies the lack of consistent feedback recording (L63)
- **Analyst 1**: provides example of discovering outdated answers weeks later (L65)

## Evidence

- **No feedback mechanism exists**: "The team currently has no consistent way of recording whether an answer produced by previous research was useful, whether it was subsequently corrected, or which source ultimately resolved the question." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L63)**
- **Outdated-answer discovery**: "Analysts sometimes discover after several weeks that a previous answer was based on an outdated document or incomplete interpretation. There is no systematic feedback mechanism for capturing this information." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L65)**
- **Risk of incorrect interpretations**: "The SME added that the corresponding risk is not simply wasted time. An incomplete search, outdated source or misunderstood requirement can lead to an incorrect interpretation being circulated internally." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L101)**
- **Desire for feedback mechanisms implied**: The manager's stated "desired outcome is to improve consistency between analysts" (L67) presupposes some feedback mechanism to track consistency over time.

## Contradicting evidence

- **Lack of explicit feedback request**: No direct request for a feedback mechanism is stated; L63 only documents the absence of one, and L75–L77 focuses on time-savings rather than feedback loops.
- **No decision on feedback handling**: L111 records "No decision was made...on...success metrics" — whether feedback handling is a success criterion is undetermined.
