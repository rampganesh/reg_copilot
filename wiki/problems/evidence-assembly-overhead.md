# Evidence Assembly Overhead

## Statement

Analysts spend **5 minutes** understanding the answer but **15–20 minutes** finding the exact section that supports it and presenting the evidence clearly. The desired outcome is "not to replace the regulatory SME" but to enable analysts to reach the SME with "better-prepared questions and stronger evidence" (L41–L42). **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L33, L35, L41, L42)**

## Who has it

- **Analyst 1**: quantified the split between understanding and evidence-gathering (L33)
- **Analyst 2**: states "simply returning a regulatory document would not solve the problem" (L35)
- **Manager**: wants "analysts to reach the SME with better-prepared questions and stronger evidence" (L41)
- **SME**: distinguishes "finding information" from "interpreting information" (L37)

## Evidence

- **Time split**: "The analyst may spend five minutes understanding the answer and another fifteen or twenty minutes finding the exact section that supports it and presenting the evidence clearly." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L33)**
- **Document-returning is insufficient**: "Analyst 2 said that simply returning a regulatory document would not solve the problem. Analysts need help getting from a broad document to the specific section, paragraph, table, reporting instruction or definition relevant to the question." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L35)**
- **Finding vs. interpreting**: "the distinction between **finding information** and **interpreting information** is important. The team would welcome assistance with finding and organising evidence, but they would not want an AI system to present an uncertain interpretation as a regulatory conclusion." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L37)**
- **Desired outcome**: "the desired outcome is not to replace the regulatory SME. The team wants analysts to reach the SME with better-prepared questions and stronger evidence when escalation is necessary." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L41)**
- **Evidence-gathering is valuable but time-consuming**: "When an analyst responds to a question, they often include links, document references, extracts or screenshots so that another person can independently verify the conclusion. This evidence-gathering is valuable but time-consuming." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L31–L34)**
- **Speed vs. accuracy**: "Analyst 2 said that speed is important, but accuracy and trust are more important for regulatory work. They would rather spend another minute checking an answer than use a fast answer that cannot be verified." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L69)**

## Contradicting evidence

- **High evidence-gathering value**: L31 states evidence-gathering is "valuable" but L35 says it's "time-consuming" and "the analyst may spend...fifteen or twenty minutes" on it, suggesting efficiency pressure.
- **Accuracy vs. speed trade-off**: L69 explicitly prioritizes accuracy over speed, yet the core problem the manager describes is "the amount of **time and effort required** to find, connect, validate and present regulatory information" (L99), suggesting the team seeks time savings within the accuracy constraint.
