# Version and Effective-Date Ambiguity

## Statement

During regulatory transitions, a document may describe existing requirements while another describes future requirements, and analysts must distinguish "whether they are answering a question about the current reporting regime, the future Basel 3.1 regime, or the transition between the two." An answer that is "technically correct but refers to a requirement that is not yet applicable can still be operationally wrong." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L17, L19)**

## Who has it

- **Regulatory Change/Reporting SME**: flags cross-referencing during transitions (L17)
- **Regulatory Reporting Manager**: notes the Basel 3.1 implementation creates large operational changes (L5, L55)
- **Analyst 1 & 2**: encounter situationally dependent effective dates (L19, L87)

## Evidence

- **Regime-distinguishing is required**: "A document may describe the existing requirement while another document describes a future requirement. Analysts need to know whether they are answering a question about the current reporting regime, the future Basel 3.1 regime, or the transition between the two." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L17)**
- **Effective dates are critical**: "An answer that is technically correct but refers to a requirement that is not yet applicable can still be operationally wrong." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L19)**
- **The corpus is not static**: "The regulatory material is not static. New clarifications, amendments and related publications can appear after an analyst has already performed research. The product would therefore need to deal with document dates and versions rather than treating the regulatory corpus as a permanently fixed knowledge base." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L87)**
- **Basel 3.1 timing**: "The team is currently preparing for the transition to the Basel 3.1 framework, with the largest operational changes expected around the 1 January 2027 implementation date." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L5)**
- **Reporting requirements change**: "The SME noted that the regulatory material is not static. New clarifications, amendments and related publications can appear after an analyst has already performed research." **(source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L87)**

## Contradicting evidence

- **Explicit no decision on timeline**: L111 records "No decision was made...on implementation timeline" — the stated 1 Jan 2027 date is domain background, not a commitment the system can rely on as a hard constraint.
