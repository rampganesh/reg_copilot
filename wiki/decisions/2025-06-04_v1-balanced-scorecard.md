# V1 balanced scorecard with manual-process baseline

## Context
No success metrics existed after two meetings (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L111); the manager's stated goal was reduced search/assembly time with maintained or improved quality (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L73); SMEs warned an easy-questions-only evaluation misleads (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L137).

## Options considered
1. **Usage/thumbs-up metrics only** — rejected: not enough information to improve research quality (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L87).
2. **Balanced scorecard** — adopted (source: 2025_06_04_digest_clarifications.md, row 3).

## Decision & rationale
Success covers **productivity, retrieval quality, answer/evidence quality, safe behaviour, and adoption**. Measures: time to a supported answer; retrieval relevance/recall; citation correctness and completeness; groundedness; SME usefulness; appropriate refusal/escalation; unsupported-answer rate; repeat usage and adoption. A **manual-process baseline is established first**. North-star candidate: *reduction in time required to produce a verified, evidence-backed answer without reducing evidence quality or increasing unsafe answers* (source: 2025_06_04_digest_clarifications.md, row 3).

## Reversal conditions
- If the north-star metric proves unmeasurable or gameable in practice, revisit with the metrics trigger (see Stage 2 `wiki/metrics/`).
- If safe-behaviour measures (refusal/escalation rates) create perverse incentives, rebalance the scorecard.
