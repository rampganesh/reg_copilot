# Analyst 1 (experienced)

## Positions
- **Workflow**: starts from questions from the reporting team/other teams/SMEs; internal knowledge base first, then PRA website; opens multiple documents when unclear (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L9)
- **Validation is the hard part**: first relevant document is easy to find; establishing it is the *right*, applicable, non-superseded source is the difficulty (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L11)
- **Wants normal-language queries** without needing exact source terminology (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L43)
- **Wants supporting material returned with the answer** instead of manual multi-document search (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L57)
- **Value proposition**: reduce repetitive searching and evidence collection, not eliminate expert judgement (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L77)

## Evidence
- Evidence-assembly time cost: "five minutes understanding the answer and another fifteen or twenty minutes finding the exact section that supports it and presenting the evidence clearly" (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L33)
- Experience gap: newer analysts spend considerably longer on the same answers (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L23)
- No feedback mechanism for corrected prior answers (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L65)
- Asked whether the system should determine document hierarchy (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L33)

### From interview (2025-06-17) — positions behaviourally confirmed
- End-to-end walkthrough: effort concentrated in version confirmation, cross-referencing, evidence assembly — not first-document finding (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L24)
- Six-point trust rejection list (vague citations, missing wording, unclear status, confident conclusions over diverging sources); citation useful only if it reduces verification effort (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L46-57)
- Escalation with bounded uncertainty ("these are the relevant sources, but this point requires SME confirmation"); stale-answer concerns include who received it and whether anyone acted (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L78-95)
- Would not hand over applicability judgement, unreviewed compliance interpretation, silent conflict resolution; comfortable with search/navigation/cross-referencing/initial-interpretation support (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L117-125)
- Evaluation bar: realistic difficult historical questions; trust lost quickly on confident answers with incorrect citations (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L131-143)
