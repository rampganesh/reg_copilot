# Compliance representative

No fabricated citations; no confidence scores; expose conflicts. Independently shaping product constraints.

## Positions
- **Consultations ≠ requirements**: near-identical language can be a proposal, not the operative requirement (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L13)
- **No confidence scores**: a high-confidence output does not establish applicability (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L29)
- **Interface language matters**: "The applicable requirement is..." materially different from "The relevant sources indicate..." plus evidence and caveat (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L105)
- **Never say "the regulation says"** when the evidence is a supervisory statement, explanatory publication or internal interpretation (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L39)
- **Incorrect citation worse than none**: false impression of auditability (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L55)

## Evidence
- Conflicts must be exposed rather than silently resolved — raised the question directly (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L67)
- "I don't have sufficient evidence to answer this" is an acceptable outcome (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L77)
- Internal answers must be labelled separately from regulatory sources, especially where copied into formal documentation (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L81-83)
- Auditability ≠ storing the AI response; must establish which material informed the response and whether it was current/relevant (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L93)
- Retaining final answers raises retention/ownership/reliance considerations; not assumed in initial design (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L151); evaluation should include confidently-wrong answers (source: L139)
