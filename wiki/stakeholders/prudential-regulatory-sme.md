# Prudential Regulatory SME

Authority is relational; effective dates as metadata; no auto-applicability.

## Positions
- **Regulatory Content Owner role**: responsible for identifying and confirming relevant amendments and changes to the approved regulatory corpus; technical pipeline detection does not establish relevance or authority (source: 2025_06_14_meeting_latedocuments.md, L6); validation gate for amendments: authority, status, effective date, relationships (L7)
- **PRA website is not one source of truth**: material types differ in purpose, status and authority (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L7, L9)
- **Relational hierarchy, not a ranking**: "Rulebook always wins" is unsafe; understand relationships between sources, no universal authority score (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L33-37)
- **Answers must establish three things**: what requirement, which version/period, whether applicable (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L21)
- **Applicability is context-dependent** (entity, perimeter, permissions, regime, exposure, date); no definitive applicability decisions in v1; gathering information ≠ making the determination (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L95-101)
- **Conflicts: expose, don't resolve**; AI resolution treated cautiously; sometimes escalate (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L67-71)
- **No confidence scores**: trust from transparent evidence, source status, dates, escalation (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L107)

## Evidence
- Semantic search is double-edged: conceptual retrieval needed, but similar-sounding concepts can have materially different meanings (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L43-47)
- No manufactured citations/quotations; quotations need context (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L53-57)
- Balanced refusal: unnecessary refusal also reduces usefulness (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L141)
- Consistency expected for factual retrieval; for ambiguous interpretation, surface evidence and ambiguity (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L143)
- Fallback: ambiguous behaviour may be to surface evidence rather than answer (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L143, L145); trust test for a pilot = realistic historical questions with correct sources, expert-verifiable citations, incl. insufficient-evidence cases (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L133-135)
