# Regulatory Change/Reporting SME

## Positions
- **Finding vs. interpreting distinction**: welcomes help finding/organising evidence, not AI presenting uncertain interpretation as a regulatory conclusion (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L37, L71)
- **Assistant for regulatory research, not final authority** (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L71)
- **Effective dates are critical**: a technically correct answer about a not-yet-applicable requirement is operationally wrong (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L19); current vs. future vs. transition regimes must be distinguishable (L17)
- **Risk framing**: incomplete search/outdated sources lead to incorrect interpretations circulating internally (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L101)
- **Professional judgement is unavoidable** in some questions; two analysts may differ and need an SME (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L39)

## Evidence
- Cross-referencing is especially important during transition (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L17)
- Downstream impact of change is a much larger problem than finding text (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L61)
- The corpus is not static; needs document dates/versions, not a fixed knowledge base (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L87)
- Bidirectional navigation: template → requirement and requirement → reporting representation; no single starting point (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L63-65); two use cases for experienced vs. newer analysts may need different behaviour (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L81)
