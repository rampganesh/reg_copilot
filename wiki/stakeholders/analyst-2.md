# Analyst 2

Values inspectable provenance and accuracy over speed; concerned with newer-analyst ramp-up.

## Positions
- **Show where the answer came from**: uncomfortable using AI answers without immediately inspectable sources (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L45)
- **Accuracy and trust outrank speed**: would rather spend another minute checking than use a fast unverifiable answer (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L69)
- **Prefer honest uncertainty**: "I couldn't find sufficient evidence to answer this" beats a plausible answer without strong support (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L89)
- **Drill-down help needed**: returning a whole document does not solve the problem; need document → specific section/paragraph/table/instruction/definition (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L35)

## Evidence
- Terminology/cross-referencing problem: same concept across rule/SS/instruction types with differing wording (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L13); template-field example requiring rule + definitions + prior material (L15)
- Wants automatic related-material surfacing: definitions, related instructions, relevant changes (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L51)
- Informal colleague channels fill search gaps (source: 2025_05_24_meeting_kickoff.md, L25)
- Citations should identify document, section/paragraph, and context (source: 2025_05_26_meeting_followup.md, L51); wants to know when the system is uncertain (L89); raised What-has-changed as a question type (L59)

### From interview (2025-06-18) — positions behaviourally confirmed
- Strict six-question validation routine before relying on any source (authoritative? correct version? operative status? answers the question? more specific instruction? linked provisions?); "search relevance and regulatory correctness are two different things" (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L31-42)
- Rejected research: broad citations, contextless screenshots, "PRA guidance" without location, secondary over primary sources, non-reproducible citations, status-inconsistent statements (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L50-57)
- Provenance is part of the answer itself; reporting questions need template/table/row/field/instruction-level references (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L63-75)
- Escalates documented evidence rather than picking the most plausible interpretation; "latest" labelling inadequate (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L81-98)
- "A fast answer with weak provenance would not constitute success" — success = less effort with maintained/improved auditability and reproducibility (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L145, L155)
