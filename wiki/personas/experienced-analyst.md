# Persona: Experienced analyst

Based on Analyst 1 and Analyst 2's experienced-side behaviour (evidence-chained).

## Job to be done
Answer regulatory questions with defensible, verifiable evidence — fast where possible, but verifiability is non-negotiable.

## Behaviour
- **Jumps directly to likely sources** and recognises relevant terminology without consciously following every step (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L111)
- Treats the first search result as a starting point, running a validation routine: document type, final/proposed/historical status, effective date, more-specific instructions, reference chains (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L28-38; source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L31-39)
- Assembles provenance as part of the answer, not as an add-on (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L75); can provide source chains for challenged interpretations (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L61-65)
- When stuck, escalates with a well-bounded, evidence-backed question rather than forcing a conclusion (source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L83-87)
- Teaches newer colleagues the research path rather than giving answers (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L101-109)

## Value proposition
- Reduce repetitive searching and evidence assembly; "faster research without sacrificing the analyst's ability to verify the conclusion" (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L145; source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L145)
- Trust is earned per-answer: rejects vague citations, non-reproducible references, confident conclusions over diverging sources (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L46-57; source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L50-59)

## Non-delegable
Applicability judgement, definitive compliance interpretation without review, silent conflict resolution, decisions with missing context (source: 2025_06_17_interview_analyst1exp.md, L117-125; source: 2025_06_18_interview_analyst2prov.md, L118-128)
