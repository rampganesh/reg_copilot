## Desk research  - Document versions PRA Basel 3.1

The Bank of England's Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) has rolled out its Basel 3.1 implementation package across a foundational consultation paper, subsequent near-final policy statements, final rules, and recent targeted adjustments. [1, 2]  
Key Consultation and Publication Timeline 

• Initial Consultation Paper: The PRA published CP16/22 – Implementation of the Basel 3.1 standards in November 2022, detailing comprehensive proposals for risk-weighted assets, credit risk, market risk, and the 72.5% aggregate output floor. 
• Near-Final Policy Statements: Feedback was split into two parts: PS17/23 in December 2023 (covering market/operational risk and CVA) and PS9/24 in September 2024 (covering credit risk and the output floor). 
• Final Rules Release: The PRA issued final rules under Policy Statement 1/26 (PS1/26) in January 2026. 
• Recent Adjustments: The PRA released  CP9/26 – Basel 3.1: Adjustments to the internal model approach (IMA) for market risk  in June 2026 to introduce operational simplifications and targeted adjustments. [4]  
• The subsequent publications also include a what changed document as a comparison.
